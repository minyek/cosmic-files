# #40 Move to Trash reports success when nothing was trashed [CLOSED]

## Problem

Nothing tests that a move to Trash that fails is reported as a failure. On a filesystem without a usable trash (another mount, read-only, NTFS, network), the file stays where it was, and the app must not report the operation as done.

## Cause

`Operation::Delete` (`src/operation/mod.rs`) passes on the inner `Result` of `trash::delete`, but no test pins that.

## Fix

A regression test, with an `operation_delete` helper, checks that a failed move to Trash surfaces its error.

## Tests

`delete_surfaces_error_when_trash_fails`.

## Upstream

The defect is present in pop-os/cosmic-files `master` (4efe802).
