Keep active requirements and plans in `requirements/` and `plans/`. Move them
under `archive/<topic>/` when their acceptance is complete; preserve filenames
and update relative links. Completed local-improvement plans and requirements
are in [archive/local-improvements/](archive/local-improvements/).

Keep decision rationale in `decisions/`, durable facts in `memory/`, evidence
and historical reviews in `reports/`, and instructions in `guides/` or
`runbooks/`. Historical reports describe their recorded source; their older
pending verdicts are superseded only by an explicit later acceptance report.
